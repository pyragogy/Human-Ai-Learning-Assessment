# Measurement specification — draft v0.1

## Construct map
1. Conceptual understanding: explain and apply ideas independently.
2. Critical reasoning: detect defects and justify corrections.
3. Transfer: apply to unfamiliar problems.
4. Retention: preserve capabilities over time.
5. Metacognitive calibration: confidence vs actual performance.
6. Human–AI process: verification, challenge, revision, over-reliance; these are **process indicators**, not proof of learning.

## Scoring
Use task-specific analytic rubrics (e.g. 0–3 per dimension) with exemplars and anchors. Keep pre/post forms parallel and test for difficulty comparability. Blind raters to intervention assignment and, when possible, testing phase. Assess inter-rater agreement and adjudicate disagreements. Check for differential item functioning before cross-group comparisons where sample size permits.

## Candidate epistemic quality index (EQI)
Historical/theoretical inspiration only. Specify a construct definition, observable items, scoring model, reliability and validity evidence before reporting EQI as a measurement scale. Do not infer it has existing psychometric validation.

## Minimum event log
Pseudonymous participant_id; study_arm; session_id; timestamp; task_id; event_role; event_type; content reference; model/provider/version; prompt/configuration version; consent/version; coding provenance. Do not commit raw personal dialogue or identifiable learner records to Git.

## Guardrails
Predefine missingness rules; prevent train/test contamination; record model changes; offer accessibility adaptations; ensure that student assessments remain independently authored.
