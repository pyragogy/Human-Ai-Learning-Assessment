# Initial item blueprint — prototype, not validated

**Domain for synthetic rehearsal:** interpreting experimental evidence and identifying causal fallacies, suitable for independent scoring and far transfer. This is not the final chosen curriculum.

| Task | Stage | Construct | Learner instruction | Rubric focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | T0 | causal explanation | Explain why a correlation does not establish causation | confounds, alternatives |
| E2 | T2 | causal explanation | Assess claim from a new observational example | reasoning, limits |
| E3 | T3 | far transfer | Evaluate a causal claim in an unfamiliar domain | transfer, evidence |
| E4 | T4 | retention | Independently evaluate a novel claim after delay | retained reasoning |

## Four 0–3 analytic dimensions
- **Conceptual accuracy:** 0 absent/wrong; 1 labels only; 2 mostly correct with minor gaps; 3 correct, well bounded.
- **Evidence use:** 0 absent; 1 unsupported assertion; 2 relevant evidence partly interpreted; 3 discriminates evidence, limitation, alternatives.
- **Justified reasoning:** 0 no chain; 1 weak assertion; 2 coherent but incomplete; 3 coherent and defensible inference.
- **Transfer/verification:** 0 no application/check; 1 generic check; 2 appropriate but incomplete check; 3 specific independent check and condition for revision.

Total raw 0–12; reporting 0–100 only through 100 * raw / 12, **not** an equated or psychometrically calibrated scale. T0/T2/T3/T4 must use calibrated parallel forms before real studies. Blinded dual rating plus adjudication; LLM-generated feedback may aid draft review but cannot provide the reference standard.

## Intervention fidelity
A uses matched non-AI materials; B explains and asks supportive comprehension questions; C asks for learner hypotheses, contrasts alternatives, challenges unsupported claims, invites independent verification. All arms use equal intended session time. AI content is not automatically true; do not deliberately give a learner false facts without approved safeguards and debrief.
