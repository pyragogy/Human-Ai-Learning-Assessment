# Review the kit by trying to break it

**External review status:** pending. **Owner decisions:** pending.  
No authoring agent certifies this kit. Its documentation and file checks are not scientific validation.

## A bounded reviewer assignment

At a pinned commit, read the tasks and rubric. Score the six synthetic responses without seeing anchors or provenance where feasible. Then reveal those materials and explain which inferences survive.

Questions:

1. Does the rubric describe displayed task-specific reasoning, or does it mostly reward test-taking language?
2. Can the same final answer receive the same quality ratings with different assistance histories, without misattributing competence?
3. Are short, plain-language answers treated fairly?
4. Does the strongest anchor demand justified caution without rewarding universal distrust?
5. Which distinction between levels 1, 2 and 3 cannot be applied consistently?
6. Is Task I merely a close structural analogue? What evidence would justify stronger transfer language?
7. Which claim would you remove before any human pilot?

## Failure conditions proposed for owner adjudication

| Counterexample | Why it matters | Proposed response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Identical text scores differently because its AI origin is revealed | Quality and provenance are being conflated | Separate rating from interpretation |
| Fluent generic response outranks a short supported answer | Style may dominate the target construct | Revise anchors and examples |
| Raters cannot distinguish adjacent anchors with reasons | Rubric lacks actionable boundaries | Simplify or rewrite before piloting |
| Assistance supplies all target evidence but is treated as learner competence | Construct contamination | Retain indeterminate attribution; require independent evidence |
| Task wording accidentally teaches the expected conclusion | Elicitation and intervention blur | Rewrite or justify task demands |
| Scores are treated as gains across non-comparable forms | Unsupported scale inference | Block gain analysis; investigate comparability |
| Public examples are reused as secret trial items | Exposure compromises interpretation | Develop new protected forms |

These are inspectable design failure criteria, not preregistered statistical decision thresholds.

## Minimal finding record

- Pinned commit and reviewer identity/role; disclose AI assistance and conflicts.
- Task, case and rubric version.
- Exact response excerpt and disputed rating/inference.
- Reproduction: what was visible and in which order?
- Counterexample or possible falsifier of the objection.
- Proposed revision and expected consequence.
- Owner decision: pending / accept / revise / defer / reject, with rationale.
- Separate verification: pending, with reviewer and evidence.

## What comes after this draft

Owner chooses the construct and scope; external human assessment/domain review challenges tasks and anchors. Any approved adult feasibility pilot needs a separate plan with sample-size rationale, rater design, privacy, consent, ethics determination, missingness rules and predeclared progression criteria.

P01's original baseline-adjusted T2 C-versus-B comparison remains proposed and unchanged. The conversational extension remains separate. This kit supplies neither a complete T0–T4 battery nor a participant-ready intervention.
