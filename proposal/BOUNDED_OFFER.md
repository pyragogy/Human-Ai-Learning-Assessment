# Bounded contribution statement — draft, not a submitted application

## What Pyragogy can credibly offer now
- Research hypothesis: separate *AI-assisted task performance* from *independently retained student competence*.
- Prototype methodological architecture: baseline, AI-mediated learning trace, independent post-test, transfer and retention.
- Reproducible synthetic-only integrity checks and code, not empirical learner outcome evidence.
- Formal threat model for prompt leakage, dependence, scoring circularity, non-equivalent tests and model changes.
- Open-science style documentation and candidate reproducibility package, subject to IP and privacy constraints.

## What Pyragogy cannot represent as delivered or secured
- Access to a classroom or PISA-aged participants.
- University, school or OECD affiliation.
- Existing independent psychometric validation, externally approved protocol or preregistered trial.
- Ethics committee approval, power-calculated enrolment or international comparability.
- Financially supported field deployment or 24/7 operational service.

## Candidate collaboration formats
**Track A — Methods consultation:** construct definitions, falsifiers, scoring rubric and design challenge; smallest viable collaboration.
**Track B — Engineering demonstrator:** isolated synthetic AI-interaction environment, provenance logs, export and defensive tests.
**Track C — Joint pilot (conditional):** possible only if an education/psychometrics partner explicitly provides recruitment capacity, authorisations and resources.

## Evaluation criteria for an OECD submission
Eligibility in final solicitation; demonstrable novelty relative to PISA RDI and MAIL simulated-environment work; feasible responsibilities; credible safeguarding; bounded IP ownership; clear human/financial resourcing despite no partner remuneration.

## One-sentence positioning
**We propose a reproducible, process-aware assessment methodology for distinguishing AI-assisted performance from independently demonstrated learning—not a claim that our system has already improved student outcomes.**
