## Proposal requirements in the PDF

The proposal is approximately **two pages** and addresses: (1) proposed PISA-use solution, defined task/scoring scope, and **specific AI models**; (2) collaboration activities and OECD inputs; (3) intellectual property and third-party rights; (4) privacy, AI digital security, and ensuring participant data are **not used for AI training**.

OECD weights proposals **70% technical quality / PISA relevance / digital security** and **30% scalability feasibility / provider dependency / cost**. Cooperation is **not paid procurement**; external-funding dependence may be disclosed. Eligibility of a single independent researcher is not expressly settled by the supplied document.

## HALA alignment and critical gap

**Strong methodological fit:** measuring whether student–AI conversations contain valid proficiency evidence; process-aware assessment, bias/contamination checks, independent evidence anchors, versioning, security by design.

**Major conceptual gap:** current HALA research primarily measures *independently retained learning after tutoring*. The call primarily targets *proficiency evidence elicited and scored during AI-mediated assessment interactions*. A valid proposal must center the latter, with independent post-tests/transfer as supporting validity anchors, rather than substituting them for conversational scoring.

**Readiness limits:** A/B/C demos use scripted/synthetic interactions; C has limited deterministic branching, no live model. No validated rubrics, student pilot, psychometric results, international language calibration, data-processing contract, consent authorization, institutional partner, or production security. No real student data have been collected.

## Proposed bounded contribution (conditional, not existing capabilities)

Develop **three proposed assessment tasks** in causal reasoning and evidence evaluation (task count is a new proposal). Compare an explanatory assistant and critical collaborator with equivalent constraints; collect interaction traces and blinded process-rubric ratings, plus independent tasks as triangulating anchors. Specify candidate models, offline/locally hosted fallback, token costs, latency, multilingual feasibility and whether any data are sent to third parties. Pilot recruitment, validation and cross-country comparisons require genuine partners, approvals and funding.
