## Formal scoring and proposal format (confirmed in attached PDF)

Approx. two pages: proposed assessment solution with actual AI model choices; OECD collaboration inputs; IP and third-party licences; privacy, security and non-training of learner data. OECD assessment weights: **70% technical/relevance/security**; **30% scalability, provider dependency, cost**. No remuneration under cooperation; external funding may be necessary. A HALA response must score *AI interaction processes* as well as using independent tests for validity, not replace the interaction-measurement question with retention research.
