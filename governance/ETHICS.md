# Ethics, privacy and research governance

- No real learner recruitment or data collection until appropriate ethical review/authorization, participant information and consent process are in place.
- Minors require additional safeguarding, parental/guardian permissions as applicable and institution-level procedures.
- Minimize data: pseudonymize identifiers, separate re-identification keys, encrypt restricted records, establish retention and deletion schedules.
- Document data controller/processor roles, lawful basis and GDPR obligations where applicable; complete a DPIA where required.
- Do not place personal data, conversation transcripts or API secrets in GitHub.
- Clearly label AI interactions and debrief any intentionally introduced incorrect statements. Avoid high-stakes or emotionally manipulative deception.
- Allow opt-out and withdrawal consistent with approved protocols.
- Track adverse events, accessibility, bias and disparate outcomes.
- Publication and open science: release only approved, de-identified materials and aggregate results after disclosure review.
