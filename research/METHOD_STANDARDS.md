# Standards crosswalk and evidence sources (2026-10-08)

These are methodological reference points, **not certifications, formal endorsements or proof that HALA conforms**.

| Source | Scope | Application to HALA | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [SPIRIT 2025](https://www.consort-spirit.org/published-statements) | Trial protocol reporting | Identify enrollment, interventions, outcomes, randomization, data management, harms, dissemination and amendments in P01-E | Dated protocol, populated checklist |
| [CONSORT 2025](https://www.bmj.com/content/389/bmj-2024-081123) | Randomised trial results reporting | Participant flow, all outcomes, deviations, transparency, funding, protocol/data access | Results report after trial, not now |
| [Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing](https://ncme.org/resources/books/testing-standards/) (AERA/APA/NCME, 2014) | Validity, reliability and fairness of test interpretations | Establish construct map, scoring reliability, comparison fairness and intended score uses | Measurement validation dossier |
| [What Works Clearinghouse standards briefs](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/standardsbriefs) | Education impact-study review | Attrition, baseline comparability and confounding | Randomization logs, baseline summaries, attrition |
| [OSF preregistration support](https://help.osf.io/article/330-welcome-to-registrations) | Time-stamped research plans | Freeze hypotheses, exclusions, analysis, deviations before eligible data | Public/time-stamped registration link |

## Checklist for external reviewer
1. Can the estimand be written without appealing to a post-treatment variable?
2. Is the interpretation of the T2 score defensible for the defined population?
3. Can masked raters score the same answers consistently?
4. Could prompt leakage, provider drift or unequal dose explain apparent effects?
5. Is it ethical, legally supportable and feasible to recruit the planned population?
6. Would a null finding be publishable without redefining success?

## Terminology discipline
- **Instrument draft** = proposed items/score rubric.
- **Calibrated/validated instrument** requires empirical evidence.
- **Automated checks passing** = software and data-integrity tests, not educational effectiveness.
- **Preregistered** requires a genuine dated external registration; Git commits alone are not presented as an OSF preregistration.
- **Independent review** requires identifiable third-party critique, not invented 'subagent' approval.
