# Estimands and statistical analysis plan (SAP) — draft, not preregistered

## Primary question
For the eligible population and the specified learning unit, what is the intention-to-treat effect of assignment to **critical AI collaborator C** versus **explanatory tutor B** on the independent T2 score, after adjustment for baseline T0?

## Estimand
Population: consenting adult volunteers in pilot, later independently reviewed adolescent target population. Treatment: randomized offer of C versus B, not adherence. Variable: rubric-scored independent T2 task (0–100) on a pre-specified domain. Intercurrent events: non-adherence retained in assigned arm for ITT; safety withdrawal and missing T2 reported separately. Summary: mean difference adjusted for T0, with uncertainty interval. Other arm contrasts secondary.

## Design decisions pending before recruiting
Eligibility; learning domain; operational rubrics; random sequence generation/concealment; unit of randomization; interference/contamination; practical or clinically/educationally meaningful effect; sample size from pilot variance/feasibility; handling of teacher/class clustering.

## Analytic guardrails
Pre-specify an ANCOVA / robust regression baseline model, allocation arm and other limited covariates; prespecify attrition and missingness sensitivity bounds; avoid significance claims from underpowered pilots. Record all departures in a timestamped amendment log. Never combine assisted T1 and independent T2 into an averaged 'learning' scale.

## Exploratory mediation
Process indicators during T1 (questioning, justification, verification and productive revision) can be studied as candidate mediators, but associations between post-treatment processes and outcomes are not identified causal mediation without stronger assumptions/designs.

## Measurement equivalence
Parallel forms, anchor tasks, rater blinding, inter-rater agreement, floor/ceiling, DIF and invariance are required before broad comparisons. Use partial-credit IRT only with adequate sample size and suitable items; not as a decoration in a tiny pilot.
