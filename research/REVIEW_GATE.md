# Independent review request — reviewer pack v1

**Public description:** HALA investigates learning retained after interaction with AI, not merely assisted product quality.
**Status:** protocol draft; no human data, external peer review, validated outcome or PISA affiliation.

## Reviewer assignment
Provide blinded methodological review using these prompts. Reviewers must declare expertise, institutional or financial conflicts, and which documents they examined. Save critiques as issue/approved report, without learner data.

### Psychometric reviewer
- Define T0/T2/T3/T4 construct and intended interpretation.
- Challenge rubrics, parallel-form claims, scaling of ordinal scores, rater reliability and comparability across populations.
- Specify minimum pilot evidence and conditions for blocking deployment.

### Causal methods reviewer
- Check assignment, treatment definition, intended estimand, contamination and time-on-task.
- Test whether confounding, attrition or unmeasured treatment heterogeneity could explain findings.
- Review planned power strategy, missingness bounds and outcome multiplicity.

### Learning scientist
- Test whether domain tasks capture learning rather than memorization or prompt mimicry.
- Propose near and far transfer probes, and checks on delayed retention.
- Evaluate fidelity of pedagogical contrasts.

### Ethics and governance reviewer
- Review recruitment, noncoercion, provider processing, GDPR, safeguards, deletion and adult/minor boundaries.
- Reject recruitment until defensible governance is documented.

### Red-team reviewer
- Attempt to produce artificial 'improvements' via leaked prompts, scoring bias, repeated items, model drift, participant collusion, or favorable attrition.
- Report counterexamples and replication blockers.

## Structured disposition
Reviewer ID / affiliation (if approved) / date / scope / evidence consulted / PASS-REVISE-BLOCK / critical findings / reproducible example / severity / remedy / remaining uncertainty.

These are agent-performed software reviews, **not** a qualified independent human psychometric review, study ethics clearance or institutional sign-off.
