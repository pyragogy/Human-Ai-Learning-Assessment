# HALA without a classroom — bounded research strategy
**Version:** 2026-10-08 · **Status:** proposed plan, not a validated study.

## Decision
Pyragogy does **not** currently have a classroom, recruited learners, school consent or a psychometric evaluation partner. Never imply otherwise. Therefore the project's defensible near-term contribution is **instrument development, reproducible software, methodological critique and partner-ready materials** — not a claimed effect on student learning.

## Three evidence layers
| Layer | Executable without learners? | Permitted inference | Forbidden inference |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 design and threat modelling | Yes | Design is internally specified | Design is valid or effective |
| L1 synthetic / scripted agent tests | Yes | Pipeline, data integrity, isolation, reproducibility and known failure modes under specified fixtures | Humans learn better |
| L2 expert review and approved cognitive interviews | Requires willing and qualified independent people, ethics determination as applicable | Clarity, content relevance, usability and inter-rater evidence in sampled cases | Generalisable effect on learners |
| L3 approved controlled learner study | Requires institution/recruitment/ethics | Feasibility or, if powered and preregistered, causal effects in defined population | Universal or PISA population validity without bridging studies |

**Synthetic participants are not people.** LLM-generated student responses must be explicitly labelled simulations and cannot establish human learning, reliability of a human score, or instrument validity.

## Near-term outputs we can own
1. Fully specified construct map with observable outcomes and failure conditions.
2. Frozen independent assessment item bank in isolated storage; parallel-form blueprint and scoring manual.
3. Local research demonstration with simulated sessions, event lineage, versioned tutor policies, safety constraints and analysis exports.
4. Model-neutral provider interface and full logging of model/prompt version, reproducible seeds where feasible.
5. Threat-model tests: answer leakage, intervention contamination, data loss, scoring bias, model drift and privacy.
6. Small, honest technical research note: what passed in *software*, what failed, and what remains untested with learners.

## Partnership model
**Pyragogy:** protocol, code, stimulus design, process instrumentation, open-science documentation.
**School/university/assessment partner (NOT SECURED):** recruitment, safeguarding, ethics approval, participant operations, psychometrics and implementation context.
**Jointly:** pre-registration, power analysis, real evaluation, publication and transparent IP/data agreement.

## Proposed OECD positioning
A limited, modular **methodology + technical demonstrator** supporting process-aware assessment. Not a turnkey PISA test, cross-national validated score, credentialed psychometric supplier or school deployment. The call's eligibility and full requirements must be checked independently. Avoid offering unpaid participant recruitment or regulatory/legal commitments that cannot be met.

## Decision gates
- **Now:** demo with synthetic data and reproducible threat tests.
- **Before asking independent experts:** send a concise review pack, declare any conflicts, obtain consent for their feedback.
- **Before human data:** ethics and data processing requirements documented, assessment validated for its intended pilot use.
- **Before efficacy conclusions:** randomization, independent outcomes, preregistration, power, rater reliability and explicit limitations.
