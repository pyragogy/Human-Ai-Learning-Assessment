# Hypotheses and failure conditions — pre-registration candidates

- **H1 (learning):** Critical AI collaboration (C) improves independent T2 score more than explanatory tutoring (B), controlling for T0. **Falsifier:** no meaningful between-arm difference within the pre-specified uncertainty and equivalence framework.
- **H2 (transfer):** C improves independent T3 transfer more than B. **Falsifier:** transfer gains do not exceed the comparator's within pre-specified decision bounds.
- **H3 (retention):** C has a stronger independent T4 retention outcome than B. **Falsifier:** no defensible retention advantage.
- **H4 (mechanism, exploratory):** Independently coded evidence-checking and reasoned revisions during T1 predict later independent scores beyond baseline. **Disconfirmation:** associations vanish after controls, fail replication, or show unreliable coding.

A failure of H1–H3 does not mean AI cannot support learning; it limits the tested intervention, population, tasks and context. H4 is not a causal claim without an additional design.

## Critical distinction
Assisted T1 output quality is **not** the learning endpoint. A high-quality answer produced jointly with AI cannot establish durable individual learning.
