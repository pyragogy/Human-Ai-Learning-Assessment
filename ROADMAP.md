# Research and engineering roadmap

## Phase 0 — Submission due diligence (October 2026)
Verify original OECD call, eligibility, scope, staffing and deadline. Create requirement traceability and go/no-go decision.

## Phase 1 — Minimum research package
Complete construct map, preregistration draft, validated or piloted test items, rater rubric and controlled intervention scripts.

## Phase 2 — Prototype
Implement three study conditions, consent-gated pseudonymous event logs, independent assessment screens and immutable analysis exports. Use synthetic test data until approvals.

## Phase 3 — Feasibility pilot
Recruit only with required approvals, check task appropriateness, usability, coding agreement, attrition, technical fidelity and privacy controls.

## Phase 4 — Validation
Refine instruments, study reliability and validity, power a randomized comparison if feasible, assess generalizability and publish positive/negative findings.

## Immediate decisions
Confirm learning domain, comparator definition, primary outcome, participant group, candidate collaborators, and protocol ownership.
