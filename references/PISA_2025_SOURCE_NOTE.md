The table combines questionnaire responses about checking multiple reliable sources and preferring common sense to scientific studies. Strong scientific: 46.4%; non-restrictive: 37.5%; non-scientific: 10.9%. A fourth combination is omitted from the displayed table. Source: PISA database Table I.B1.3.36.

These are reported beliefs, not observed AI fact-checking. Both of the largest groups endorse source checking; their attitude toward common sense differs. Associations with science achievement do not establish causation.

**Implication proposed for HALA:** evaluate demonstrated evidence appraisal separately from declared attitudes and assisted output quality. The report supplies context, not validation of HALA or evidence of an OECD decision about the submission.
